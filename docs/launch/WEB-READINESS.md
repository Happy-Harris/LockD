# Web readiness audit

Audited 2026-10-01 against `main` at `170c6c7`. Phase 1: documentation only; no fixes or product decisions. **Ready** means an implemented capability supported by the cited code, not certification of a live deployment. **Gap** means missing or insufficient implementation. **Cannot tell** means the repository cannot establish the outcome. Relative paths are evidence; no deployment identifiers or credentials are recorded.

The attached launch plan distinguishes guest web launch, accounts, and native distribution. Those phases differ from the older phase labels in `docs/STATUS.md`; this report uses **guest launch** and **accounts launch** to avoid ambiguity. `CLAUDE.md` governs the work. Its logging, guest/offline, honest-number and free-history principles remain requirements. No payments, subscriptions, pricing, paywalls or analytics are proposed.
